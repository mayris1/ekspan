KE / STRUCTURAL BEARINGS

KE / Pot bearings

Load transfer and rotation, with fixed, guided or free movement arrangements according to project requirements.

HOW IT WORKS

Pot bearings.
The engineering inside.

Rotation is accommodated by a moulded rubber pad contained within the pot and restrained by brass piston rings. The bearing transfers load while allowing rotation.

Movement arrangements

Fixed, guided or free arrangements allow or restrain translation according to the project requirements.

Construction details

The published range includes welded sliding surfaces and integrated guides for positioning.
