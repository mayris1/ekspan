THE PROJECT

Made in Sheffield.
Set in motion at Wichelstowe.

A bridge that connects people.
And makes way for boats.

EKSPAN manufactured and installed Bushey Fox Bridge, bringing structural steel fabrication, specialist bearing engineering and site installation together. Designed by Mason Clark Associates, the manually operated crossing serves walkers, wheelchair users and cyclists.

SHEFFIELD / MANUFACTURE & TRIAL ASSEMBLY

It starts on
the workshop floor.

The complete bridge was manufactured and assembled at EKSPAN’s Sheffield facility. Trial fitting brought the components together before the journey to Wichelstowe.

One coordinated scope, from structural steel fabrication to preparation for site installation.

WORKSHOP → TRIAL FITTING → DELIVERY
02 / DELIVERY & SITE INSTALLATION

From workshop.
To waterway.

Working for Fox (Owmby) Limited, EKSPAN delivered and installed the bridge at Wichelstowe. The installation brought the assembled structure to its canal crossing.

03 / SPECIALIST BEARING ENGINEERING

The detail that
makes the difference.

The pivot bearing is the point around which the bridge turns. EKSPAN’s installation scope included installing and grouting this bearing, then using temporary supports and jacking to set the bridge at the required level.

+

A crossing with
a moving purpose.

Manually operated, the bridge rotates on its pivot bearing into a purpose-built recess in the canal bank, allowing boats to pass.
