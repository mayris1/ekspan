DE / STRUCTURAL BEARINGS

DE / Line rocker bearings

Load transfer with rotation about a single axis.

HOW IT WORKS

Rotation. One clear axis.

DE rocker bearings transfer load while accommodating rotation about a single axis. Translation can be allowed or restrained to suit the application.

Movement to suit

Specify the required translation and restraint alongside loads and rotation.

Beyond the standard range
